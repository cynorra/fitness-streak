Account Deletion

Last updated: September 8, 2026

You can request permanent deletion of your VitaTribe account and all associated data at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed. This page describes how to do that and exactly what gets removed.

How to Request Deletion

Option 1 — From inside the app

Open VitaTribe and go to Profile → Delete Account, and follow the instructions shown there.

Option 2 — By email (no app required)

Send an email to support@vitatribe.com from the address linked to your account (or including your username), with the subject “Account Deletion Request”.

Request Account Deletion ✉️

We will verify your identity and process your request within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete.

What Gets Deleted

Once your request is processed, the following data is permanently and irreversibly deleted:

  • Account data: email address, username, and password.
  • Profile data: display name, profile photo, bio, and role.
  • Workout and nutrition data: workout logs, streak history, nutrition/diet plans, body measurements, and dietitian appointments.
  • Social data: friend connections, kudos, badges, posts, comments, and in-app messages.
  • Location history: any stored workout routes and GPS data.
  • Device data: push-notification tokens and advertising identifiers linked to your account.

Data We May Retain

Some data may be retained for a limited period beyond deletion where required by law (e.g. financial or billing records for tax/accounting purposes) or to resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. This retained data is kept only as long as legally necessary and is not used for any other purpose.

Questions

For any questions about this process, contact us at support@vitatribe.com. See our full Privacy Policy for more details on how we handle your data.
