Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

VitaTribe (“the App”, “we”) processes your personal data as data controller in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”) and Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (“KVKK”). This policy explains what data is collected when you use the VitaTribe mobile app and website, how it is used, who it is shared with, and what rights you have.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account data: email address, username, password (stored encrypted).
  • Profile data: name, profile photo, role (user/dietitian), and any other information you add to your profile.
  • Workout and nutrition data: workout logs, streak history, nutrition/diet plans, dietitian appointments and packages.
  • Social data: friends list, kudos interactions, leaderboard data, and in-app messages.
  • Location data: with your permission, we collect precise (fine) location and, optionally, background location to measure outdoor workouts (distance, route, pace). Background location is only used while an active workout is being tracked.
  • Notification data: a device push-notification token, used to send you reminders.
  • Advertising and device identifiers: a mobile advertising ID (AAID/IDFA) and technical/device data (OS, app version) used to show ads via Google AdMob.

2. Purpose and Legal Basis of Processing

Your data is processed to create and authenticate your account, provide streak/workout/nutrition tracking, enable social features (kudos, leaderboard, messaging), operate dietitian appointments and packages, send reminder notifications, and keep the app free through ad revenue. Processing relies on performance of a contract, your explicit consent, and our legitimate interests, per GDPR Art. 6 and KVKK Art. 5.

3. User-Generated Content

Content you share on your profile or with friends (workout posts, messages, profile details) is made visible according to your own choices. You control who can see this content through the app’s settings; you are responsible for the visibility choices you make, but we remain responsible for keeping your data secure and apply industry-standard security measures to that end.

4. Third Parties We Share Data With

  • Supabase: provides our authentication, database, and file storage infrastructure; your data is hosted on servers in the EU (Frankfurt) region.
  • Google AdMob: used to display in-app ads. Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are asked for ad consent via Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP); you can manage personalized ads at any time from the in-app consent screen or via Google Ad Settings.
  • Google Maps / location services: used to display your workout route on a map.

Beyond the above, your data is only shared with authorities when legally required (court order, official request). We never sell your data.

5. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies for session management. Third-party cookies placed via AdMob/AdSense may serve personalized ads based on your prior visits; you can disable these through your browser settings or the Google Ad Settings page linked above.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained while your account is active, and deleted within 30 days of an account deletion request. Data subject to a legal retention obligation (e.g. accounting records) is kept for the period required by applicable law.

7. Your Rights

Under GDPR and KVKK Art. 11, you have the right to learn whether your data is being processed, request information about such processing, learn its purpose, know the third parties it is transferred to (domestically or abroad), request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data, request its deletion or destruction, and request that these actions be notified to third parties the data was transferred to. You can exercise these rights by contacting us at the address below.

8. Account and Data Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, use Profile > Delete Account inside the app, or email support@vitatribe.com directly. Once we receive your request, all your data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

9. Children’s Privacy

VitaTribe is not directed at individuals under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If we become aware that we have collected data from a user under 16, we will delete it immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via an in-app notification or email. The current version is always published on this page.

11. Contact

For questions, requests, or complaints regarding privacy, contact us at support@vitatribe.com.
