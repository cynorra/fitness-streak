# DietitianFit — Google Ads Metinleri (App campaign, Philippines / English)

Kampanya adı: **DietitianFit - PH - Installs** (eski ad: "VitaTribe - PH - Installs"; hesabı elle güncelle).
Google App kampanyası: başlık ≤30, açıklama ≤90 karakter.

## Başlıklar (≤30)
- DietitianFit: Fitness Workout (29)
- Book Real Dietitians (20)
- Weight Loss Meal Plans (22)
- Gym & Workout Tracker (21)
- Dietitian + Workout App (23)
- Find a Dietitian Near You (25)
- Keep Streaks With Friends (25)

## Açıklamalar (≤90)
- Book certified dietitians and track your gym workouts in one free app. (70)
- Weight loss plans, meal tracking and 1:1 chat with real dietitians. (66)
- Build daily streaks, join challenges and train with friends. (59)
- Free app: dietitian appointments, diet plans and workout log. (60)

## Görseller
- 1:1, 4:5, 1.91:1 ve 9:16 için `store-assets/en-US/v2/*-1080x1920.png` ve `feature-graphic-1024x500.png` kullanılabilir.

- Eski StreakFit/VitaTribe logolu görselleri (`store-assets/google ads assets/`) kampanyadan çıkar.
